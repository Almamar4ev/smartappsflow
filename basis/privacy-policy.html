Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Basis is a calculator. It does not create an account, and it does not send what you type to a server.

What stays on this device

  • The numbers you enter, so they are still there when you come back.
  • Named calculations you choose to save for average cost and compound growth. They stay on this device.
  • Your choices for language, currency, and light or dark appearance.

Clearing the browser or app storage removes them. Uninstalling the Android app does the same.

What we do not collect

No name, no email, no trades, no analytics, and no advertising identifier. The calculators run on the device.

Basis Pro purchase

Basis Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play in the Android app. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play, on this device, whether your Google account owns Basis Pro, and keeps only that yes or no on the device. We do not send it to any server of ours.

Basis حاسبات تعمل على جهازك. لا تنشئ حسابا، ولا ترسل الارقام التي تكتبها إلى خادم.

يحفظ التطبيق محليا الارقام التي تدخلها، والحسابات التي تختار حفظها في متوسط الشراء والفائدة المركبة، واختيار اللغة والعملة والمظهر الفاتح او الداكن. مسح بيانات التطبيق او المتصفح يحذفها. لا نجمع اسما ولا بريدا ولا صفقات ولا معرفات اعلانية.
