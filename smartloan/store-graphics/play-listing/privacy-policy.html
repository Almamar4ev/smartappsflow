Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

🔒 Your financial data never leaves your device. emiGo: EMI & Loan performs all calculations locally. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal or financial information.

1. Information We Collect

emiGo does not require an account and does not collect personally identifiable information. The figures you enter (income, expenses, loan amounts, interest rates) are used only to perform calculations within the app.

2. How Your Data Is Used

All calculations and financial analysis are performed entirely on your device. Your data is used solely to provide you with loan calculations, comparisons, and financial insights within the app.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). This data:

  • Remains on your device at all times
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Can be deleted at any time by clearing the app data
  • Is not shared with any third parties

Optional backup files you export are saved by you (to your device or a share target you choose). Those files stay under your control; we never receive them.

4. No Tracking or Advertising

The app contains no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no user profiling. We do not track your activity, and we do not collect any analytics about how you use the app.

Our website may use a privacy-focused, cookie-free service to count aggregate page visits. This applies to the website only, never to the app, and it does not identify individual visitors or access any financial information.

5. Permissions and Internet Access

The app functions fully offline. No personal or financial data entered in the app is transmitted over the internet. Internet access is used only if you choose to send feedback via your own email app, or to load a web update of the Progressive Web App.

6. Children's Privacy

emiGo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

This app was previously published as Smart Loan Calculator. The privacy practices described here apply to both names.
