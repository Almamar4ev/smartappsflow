Full description — English
--------------------------
emiGo is a private loan calculator for EMI, personal loans, car loans, and mortgages.

Enter your income, expenses, amount, rate, and period. emiGo shows the monthly payment, total interest, total cost, and whether the installment stays within your bank limit.

WHAT YOU GET
• Instant EMI and loan estimates
• Bank installment limit vs your monthly payment
• Amortization schedule
• Charts for principal and interest
• Scenario comparison against an active loan
• Save and compare loans on Home
• Demo mode with sample numbers so your real file stays closed

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
Your numbers stay on your device. There is no account and no cloud login.

emiGo is a calculation and estimation tool. It does not approve loans or replace your bank’s offer.
