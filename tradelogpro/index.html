Formulas & Definitions
Analytics Definitions
Win Rate
Win Rate = (Winning Trades ÷ Total Closed Trades) × 100
Percentage of closed trades that were profitable. Evaluate it together with Avg Win/Loss (realized), not alone.
Avg Win/Loss (Realized)
Avg Win/Loss = Average Win ÷ Average Loss
From closed trades only: average winning P&L divided by average losing P&L (absolute). This is a realized outcome ratio — not the planned Risk:Reward from Entry / Stop / Target on a single trade. A value of 2 means your average win was about twice your average loss.
Profit Factor
Profit Factor = Gross Profit ÷ Gross Loss
Closed trades only (realized). Gross Profit = sum of winning trade P&L; Gross Loss = absolute sum of losing trade P&L. Open/unrealized P&L is excluded. Above 1.0 = net profitable sample; very high values usually mean few or tiny losses in the period.
ROI on Invested Trade Value
ROI = (Net P&L ÷ Total Invested) × 100
Net P&L of closed trades divided by the capital tied up in those trades (invested trade value). This is not the same as account return on deposits/withdrawals or starting capital shown on the Dashboard.
Net P&L
Net P&L = Gross P&L − Fees
Your actual profit or loss after subtracting all brokerage fees and commissions.
Avg Entry Price (Dollar Cost Averaging)
Avg Entry = Total Cost ÷ Total Shares
Total Cost = Σ (Price × Qty) for each buy
When you buy the same stock multiple times at different prices, your average entry is the weighted average across all purchases.
Unrealized P&L
Unrealized P&L = (Current Price − Avg Entry) × Remaining Shares × Direction
P&L on positions still open. Direction = +1 for Long, −1 for Short. Not realized until you close.
Calculator Formulas
P&L Calculator
Long: P&L = (Exit − Entry) × Qty
Short: P&L = (Entry − Exit) × Qty
% Change uses the same direction
Quick profit/loss estimate. Choose Long or Short in the calculator. Fees are not included here — see a trade's full Net P&L in its details.
Position Size — Mode 1 (SL in %)
Risk Amount = Balance × Risk%
Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ SL%
The default, simpler mode. Use when you know your stop loss as a percentage from entry rather than an exact price. In Cash accounts the position is capped at your balance; in Margin it shows the leverage required.
Position Size — Mode 2 (SL/TP in price)
Risk Amount = Balance × Risk%
Risk Per Share = |Entry − Stop Loss|
Shares = Risk Amount ÷ Risk Per Share
Position Size = Shares × Entry Price
Calculates exactly how many shares to buy so that if your stop loss is hit, you only lose your defined risk amount. Enter an optional Take Profit to also see Risk : Reward.
Compound Growth
Balance(month) = Balance(prev) × (1 + r) + Deposit
where r = Annual Rate ÷ 12
Shows how your account grows month by month with a consistent return rate. The power of compounding means small consistent gains add up significantly over time.
Trading Glossary
Long
Buying a stock expecting the price to go UP. You profit when price rises above your entry.
Short
Selling a stock you don't own, expecting the price to go DOWN. You profit when price falls below your entry.
Stop Loss (SL)
A price level where you exit a losing trade to prevent further losses. Essential for risk management.
Take Profit (TP)
A target price where you exit a winning trade to lock in profits.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in your account balance. Maximum drawdown is the largest drop from a high point.
Partial Close
Closing only a portion of your position (e.g. 50% of shares) to lock in some profit while keeping exposure.
