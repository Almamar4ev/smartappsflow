Full description
----------------
Edgeory is an offline-first trading journal for serious traders.

Track every trade across multiple accounts. See net P&L, win rate, average win/loss, profit factor, ROI, and equity curve in one place — without creating an account or sending your journal to the cloud.

WHAT YOU GET
• Trade log with wins, losses, and open positions
• Dashboard KPIs: balance, net P&L, win rate, avg win/loss, fees
• P&L calendar — green and red days at a glance
• Analytics: streaks, best/worst trades, ROI, profit factor
• Daily journal for emotions, notes, and discipline tags
• Built-in trading calculators: P&L, position size, compound growth, stock average
• Multi-account management with backup and restore
• Export reports to Excel or PDF
• Import trades from Trading 212 CSV

BUILT FOR FOCUS
Edgeory runs on your device. Your trades, notes, and photos stay local. Use it as a stock trade log for swing trading or day-to-day journaling — then review performance when the market is closed.

IMPORTANT
Edgeory is a personal journal and analytics tool only. It does not provide investment advice, trading signals, or brokerage services. Past performance metrics do not guarantee future results.

Start logging. Know your edge. Trade with a clearer memory of what worked.
